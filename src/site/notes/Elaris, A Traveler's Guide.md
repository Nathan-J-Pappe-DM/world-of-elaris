Welcome to Elaris, a continent of vast distances, ancient mysteries, thriving cities, and untamed wilderness. Stretching across a land comparable in size to entire nations, Elaris is home to countless villages, towns, trade routes, forests, mountains, and forgotten places that remain unexplored even in the modern age. Most people spend their entire lives within a few hundred miles of where they were born, yet tales of distant lands and hidden wonders travel constantly along the roads and waterways that connect the realm.

To many citizens, Elaris is experiencing a golden age. The great cities cooperate more often than they conflict, trade flourishes across the continent, and knowledge flows more freely than at any time in recorded history. Roads are maintained, caravans travel safely, and opportunities exist for those willing to seek them. Though dangers remain in the wild places beyond civilization, many believe there has never been a better time to live.

Yet Elaris remains a world filled with unanswered questions. Ancient ruins stand in forgotten valleys. Strange monuments rise from empty plains. Dungeons emerge in places where no structures existed before. Explorers, scholars, and adventurers continue to discover wonders that challenge long-held assumptions about the world and its history. Even after centuries of study, there are still regions that no map fully captures and mysteries that no scholar has successfully explained.

Among the greatest of these mysteries are the Scrolls of Advenire.

No one knows who created the Scrolls of Advenire or why they continue to appear throughout the world. Some are discovered within ancient ruins buried beneath mountains. Others are recovered from hidden vaults, forgotten temples, abandoned fortresses, or newly emerged dungeons. The appearance of a new dungeon often draws immediate attention from local governments, guilds, temples, and adventuring companies, all eager to recover any Scrolls that may be found within.

Despite generations of study, the Scrolls remain poorly understood. What is known is that they play a central role in society throughout Elaris. When citizens reach adulthood, they participate in a sacred ceremony known as the Inceptio Pars Confirmation, commonly referred to simply as "The Confirmation."

During the Confirmation, a Scroll of Advenire reveals an individual's Inner Path. In that moment, the recipient gains abilities and talents associated with a particular calling. Some become warriors, others scholars, healers, explorers, priests, craftsmen, performers, or countless other paths represented throughout the world. Most people view the revelation of their Inner Path as one of the defining moments of their lives.

Entire communities gather to celebrate Confirmations. Families travel great distances to witness them. Festivals often accompany the ceremonies, filling town squares with music, food, and celebration. Across Elaris, the Inner Path is widely regarded as a reflection of a person's deepest nature and an important guide for their future role within society.

The roads of Elaris connect nine great cities, each possessing its own culture, history, and identity.

Far to the northwest, among the towering Ironpeaks, stands Stonehaven. Carved directly into the mountains themselves, Stonehaven is renowned for its resilience, military tradition, and nearly impregnable defenses. Its people are known for their discipline and determination, and the city has endured hardships that would have broken lesser settlements. Travelers heading into the western mountains often begin their journeys beneath Stonehaven's watchful walls.

To the north lies Winterhold, the last great city before the frozen wilderness of the Frostcrown. Long winters and harsh conditions have shaped a people famous for their toughness and self-reliance. The city serves as both frontier outpost and guardian of the northern reaches, where snow-covered valleys and icy plains stretch toward the horizon.

Along the northeastern coast rise the cliffs of Ravensreach. Perched above crashing waves and endless sea winds, Ravensreach has become synonymous with exploration and discovery. Cartographers, sailors, adventurers, and treasure hunters all pass through its harbors. The city's citizens have an enduring fascination with what lies beyond the edge of the known world.

To the west, the fertile lands of the Golden Plains surround Goldmeadow. Endless fields of grain, orchards, vineyards, and ranches make the region one of the most productive agricultural areas on the continent. Goldmeadow's people value family, tradition, and community, and many of the realm's largest Confirmation celebrations take place beneath its open skies.

At the heart of Elaris stands Dawnmarket, the largest inland city on the continent. Located where the major roads converge, Dawnmarket serves as the commercial crossroads of the realm. Merchants, adventurers, diplomats, and travelers from every region eventually pass through its gates. The city never truly sleeps, and its markets are famous for offering goods and information from every corner of Elaris.

To the east, hidden beneath the vast canopy of the Greenwood Expanse, lies Moonhollow. Built among ancient trees and surrounded by dense forest, the city is known for its herbalists, rangers, scholars of nature, and deep respect for the natural world. The Greenwood remains one of the largest forests in Elaris, and many secrets are said to lie beneath its leaves.

On the southwestern coast stands Seabreak, the largest port city in the world. Ships from distant shores crowd its immense harbor, carrying exotic goods, strange stories, and ambitious travelers. Seabreak serves as the gateway between Elaris and the wider world, and its influence can be felt far beyond its shores.

South of Dawnmarket, among the rolling hills of the Crownlands, rises Highcrest. The city serves as the political heart of Elaris and is home to many of its most important institutions. Grand academies, government halls, archives, and public works fill its districts. High Chancellor Vex Doriane resides in Highcrest, and many citizens regard him as one of the most influential leaders of the modern age.

Far to the southeast, within the rugged mountains of the Emberlands, lies Ashwick. The city is famed for its mines, foundries, and master craftsmen. The sound of hammer against anvil echoes through its streets day and night as artisans create some of the finest weapons, armor, tools, and machinery in Elaris.

Connecting these cities are the great roads of the continent. The King's Road runs from Winterhold through the heart of civilization toward Highcrest. The Explorer's Way stretches northeast toward Ravensreach and the sea beyond. The Greenwood Path winds beneath ancient trees toward Moonhollow. The Merchant Coastway links Highcrest and Seabreak, while the Iron Road carries ore and industry between Ashwick and Dawnmarket. Countless smaller roads branch from these routes, reaching villages, settlements, and distant frontiers.

The rivers of Elaris are equally important. The mighty Aurel River is often called the Lifeblood of Elaris, feeding farms and settlements throughout the central regions. The Silverrun descends from the Ironpeaks toward the western sea. The Greenflow winds through the Greenwood Expanse, while the Emberwash carries mineral-rich waters from the mountains of Ashwick. In the south, the Crownwater nourishes the fertile lands surrounding Highcrest.

Beyond the roads and cities lie places that have inspired stories for generations. Travelers speak of the Spiral Stones standing alone upon the plains, their purpose long forgotten. High within the Ironpeaks rests the Fallen Observatory, where scholars once studied the heavens. The Silent Chasm cuts across the southeastern wilderness, swallowing sound itself. Deep within the forests stands the Glasswood, where strange crystal-barked trees shimmer in filtered sunlight. Offshore near Seabreak lie the submerged ruins known as the Drowned Crown. The Giant's Stair ascends impossibly high into the mountains. The Black Vault waits alone upon an empty plain west of Dawnmarket. The Weeping Arch spans a canyon in the south, shedding water from no visible source.

Some dismiss these places as curiosities. Others devote their lives to studying them.

For adventurers, they are invitations.

For while Elaris may be prosperous and civilized, vast stretches of the continent remain untamed. New dungeons continue to appear. Forgotten ruins await discovery. Ancient mysteries remain unsolved. Every year, adventurers leave the safety of the cities in pursuit of fortune, knowledge, glory, or purpose.

Most never become famous.

Some never return.

But every legend begins somewhere.

And in Elaris, there are always new paths waiting to be discovered.
